After an instruction from the communications minister, Nepal Telecom said residents in affected areas would get **unlimited voice, 2 GB data per day and 50 SMS for two days**. Ncell announced **three days of free voice and data** in flood-hit parts of Rasuwa, plus 100 SMS.

This is relief traffic, not a substitute for repairing fiber and microwave. It does not change the northern CTGNet session or the Galchhi corridor cut.
