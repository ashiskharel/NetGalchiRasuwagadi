Nepal Telecom told Ratopati that the 26 August Bhotekoshi flood damaged towers and the **optical fiber from Galchhi toward Rasuwagadhi**. Preliminary figures: two towers in Rasuwa and five in Nuwakot physically damaged, plus widespread power loss.

Fiber was reported **still in service as far as Trishuli**. North of that, NTC said it was keeping sites up with **microwave radio** rather than letting the corridor go fully dark. Maintenance teams were already in the field.

This is a last-mile / backhaul restoration story as well as a northern-transit story. The China session at Rasuwagadhi–Kerung is a separate question from whether Kathmandu can reach that border on fiber.
