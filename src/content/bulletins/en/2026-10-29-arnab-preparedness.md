Update, 31 October: the casualty table has been taken off this board. The check page now compares forecasts with measured rain and river level.

Tonight’s ground log is preparedness. NDRRMA, on 27 October, said effects on Nepal begin the night of Thursday 29 October 2026 and asked people to stay on high alert. On 29 October the Disaster Response Command Post met. Department spokesperson Bibhuti Pokharel said the cyclone would have weakened before it reached Nepal, and that rainfall, snowfall and moderate winds were still possible. Agencies were told to stay ready to move. No team count was published.

No Nepal figure for deaths, injuries, missing people, displacement, houses, evacuations, or named rescue teams is in the reports logged today. A blank cell is not zero. Evacuation and rescue-team figures from Indian states are not listed here. This is a different incident from the August 2026 Rasuwa Bhote Koshi flood and the Galchhi–Rasuwagadhi fiber cut.

The Meteorological Forecasting Division, via Business 360 at 12:38 Nepal time, called the day generally cloudy, with rain and snow likely later, including heavy rain or snow at one or two places in the Koshi, Bagmati and Gandaki hills tonight. When this was written, this board had no report that rain had started. That is not a rain-gauge observation, and it is too early to mark the night forecast wrong.

The GDACS/JTWC track still ends in India, as a forecast depression. It is not extended into Nepal. A separate tally row records the reading that the center itself moves into Nepal. That row is holding, not a miss. Official rain claims and the three model calls for the Bagmati Terai point stay pending until a cited observation exists.

Helplines, as Nepal News quoted an NDRRMA notice on 27 October: 1155 weather, 1234 NEOC/DEOC, 100 Nepal Police, 1114 Armed Police Force, 1144 Tourist Police. Business 360 on 28 October lists the same set without 1114. The ndrrma.gov.np page was not opened. Those numbers are not an instruction from this board.

The log runs through 7 November. Later days are empty on purpose. The tally is updated by hand after each window closes.
