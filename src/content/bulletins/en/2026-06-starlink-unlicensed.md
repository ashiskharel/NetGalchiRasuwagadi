Starlink’s director told the communications minister in June 2026 that the company wants to operate in Nepal but is **still waiting on licensing**, including a request for 100% foreign ownership of the licence. NTA has separately warned against **unauthorised terminals** (including at mountain camps).

This board records Starlink as **unlicensed**, not as an official NTC or government backup. Field reports of dishes in the flood zone belong on the Field reports page and must not be treated as a public restoration plan.
