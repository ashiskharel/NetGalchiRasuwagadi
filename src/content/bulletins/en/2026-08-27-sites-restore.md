By 27 August NTC said **152 sites** had been affected at peak and **60 were still out**: Rasuwa 13 of 32, Nuwakot 31 of 79, Dhading 13 of 36, Chitwan 3 of 5. OnlineKhabar put combined NTC + Ncell tower disruption at 87 across the three hill districts, after many of 152 NTC sites had already been brought back.

Generators and batteries were **airlifted to the Nyaupanechaur and Ramche repeaters**. NTC listed restored coverage around Kispang (Kaule), Thulegaun, Tadi, Fikuri, Kalikasthan, Grang, Dadagaun, Mailung, Pangsang Danda, Langtang and Kyangjin. Work with generators and fuel continued at Dhunche, Madanpur, Battar, Bidur Buspark, Baireni and Thamel Danda.
