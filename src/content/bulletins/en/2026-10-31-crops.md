The crop page keeps three kinds of evidence apart: a weather call, a loss written in a news report, and a satellite view of a box you draw. Millimetres of rain are not a loss. A blank is not zero.

World Bank indicator AG.LND.AGRI.K2 puts Nepal’s agricultural land at 37,441 km² in 2024. That is arable land, permanent crops, and permanent pasture. It is not a plot map and not a government allotment.

An open Sentinel dataset for 2017–2024 maps glacier-fed lakes in Nepal and the transboundary catchments. In 2024 that count was 1,046 lakes in Nepal (60.21 km²), 954 in China (90.84 km²), and 34 in India (1.13 km²). Four lakes are pinned, and only where that paper prints a coordinate: Lower Barun, Imja, Lumding, and Rongbuk. Tsho Rolpa, Thulagi, and Cirenmaco are named in a 31 August Nepal News explainer and are not pinned. The explainer’s 47-lake list is a 2020 hazard class, not an outburst notice for today. No live outburst notice was opened for these pins on 31 October 2026. A box from a pin is about 0.02° around the published point. It is not the lake shore.

News rows stayed only when the publication date and the body agreed. Kathmandu Post, 9 October, quotes local offices on late-September rain and wind in Kailali, Bardiya, Salyan, and Dang. Those figures are not added together. Kantipur, 30 October, has the Palpa Agriculture Knowledge Centre’s preliminary total of Rs 17,101,000 for Asoj 8–11. Kathmandu Post, 30 October, says about 25 percent of vegetables in Aamachhodingmo had already rotted after the 26 August Bhotekoshi flood cut the road. That flood is not Cyclone Arnab, and last year’s sales are not this year’s loss. Republica, 31 October, quotes the Meteorological Forecasting Division asking people to avoid agricultural work, including rice harvesting, on 31 October and 1 November. That row is an advisory. It has no hectare or rupee figure.

There is no national total. On 28 October the ministry secretary told a National Assembly committee the crop-loss assessment was not finished. One hail page had dates that did not agree, so it is not on the board. Saturday afternoon’s rain is not written here as a finished loss. Gauges stay on the water page.

The map preview is NASA VIIRS for 30 October 2026. A pixel is hundreds of metres, and cloud can hide the ground. It does not show a paddy field.
